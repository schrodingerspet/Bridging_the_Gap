Maharashtra Farmers Schemes

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

PM-KISAN provides direct income support to small and marginal farmer families, helping them with their agricultural and domestic needs.

Eligibility:
  • All landholding farmer families are eligible, subject to certain exclusion criteria.
Official Site

Soil Health Card Scheme

This scheme provides farmers with a Soil Health Card which contains crop-wise recommendations of nutrients and fertilizers to improve productivity.

Eligibility:
  • Available to all farmers upon request for soil sample testing.
Official Site

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

PMFBY is a crop insurance scheme providing financial support to farmers suffering crop loss or damage arising out of unforeseen events.

Eligibility:
  • All farmers including sharecroppers and tenant farmers growing notified crops in notified areas are eligible.
Official Site

Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme

The KCC scheme aims at providing adequate and timely credit support from the banking system to farmers for their cultivation and other needs.

Eligibility:
  • All farmers, individuals, or joint borrowers who are owner cultivators are eligible.
Official Site

Mahadbt Farmer Schemes

A unified portal by the Government of Maharashtra for farmers to apply for various schemes related to agriculture, including subsidies on seeds and equipment.

Eligibility:
  • Farmers must be residents of Maharashtra and registered on the Mahadbt portal.
Official Site
